Custom Stonework FAQs

What San Diego homeowners ask before we start carving.

Artificial rock is a hand-cast, concrete-based material designed to look like natural stone. It's lightweight, durable, and can be carved, colored and finished to match real stone at a fraction of the cost and weight — ideal for San Diego retaining walls, landscape features, and interior accent walls.
Yes. If your existing artificial rock has faded or you want to change the color, we can recolor it with professional stone dye and sealer that penetrates and lasts. We assess condition on site and recommend the best recoloring method for your stonework.
We carve custom designs, monograms, family names, architectural details, ornamental motifs, and decorative patterns into both artificial and natural stone. Popular requests include entry monograms, fireplace surrounds, and landscape feature walls.
Quality engineered artificial stone is built to last 15+ years in San Diego's climate — coastal salt air, UV, and moisture. Properly sealed, it resists staining and weathering. It's also lighter than natural stone, which matters for hillside builds and load-sensitive structures.
Yes. Lightweight artificial stone is ideal for interior accent walls in living rooms, dens, bedrooms and offices. It adds warmth and architectural character without the weight, structural load, and cost of real stone.
Cost depends on scope, materials, and finish complexity. Artificial stone costs less than natural stone. Carved details, recoloring, and installation labor vary by project. We provide a free estimate before any work begins — no ballparks, no surprises.
